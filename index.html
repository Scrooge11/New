How a job goes

Four steps, no runaround.

  1. Call or send the form

    Tell us what is going on. For repairs, we can usually give you a ballpark on the phone.

  2. Site visit and written quote

    We look at the panel, the run, and the finish work, then send a fixed quote with scope and timeline.

  3. Work, permit, inspection

    We pull the permit, do the work, and meet the inspector. You do not have to be the go-between.

  4. Walkthrough and cleanup

    We show you what was done, label the panel, and leave the space cleaner than we found it.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Massachusetts master electrician Lic. #00000000, home improvement contractor HIC #000000, general liability and workers' compensation. Certificates on request.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, for every job that needs one. The permit, the inspection and meeting the inspector are included in the quote.

How fast can you come out?

Repairs are usually scheduled within the week, emergencies the same day when we can. Installs are scheduled after the site visit, typically within two to three weeks.

How do you price work?

Installs and remodels get a fixed written quote after a site visit. Troubleshooting is a stated first-hour rate, then hourly plus materials, with a quote before anything bigger.

Where do you work?

Based in Westford, MA, serving Westford, Chelmsford, Littleton, Acton, Groton, Tyngsborough, Dunstable, Carlisle, Boxborough, Ayer, Harvard, Lowell, Concord, Pepperell, Billerica and Bedford, and about 30 miles around for larger projects.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. Outlets, switches, a ceiling fan, a bathroom fan, a dead circuit. Small jobs are how most customers meet us.

What warranty do you offer?

A 2-year workmanship warranty on our work, plus the manufacturer warranty on equipment we install.

How do payments work?

Service calls are paid at completion. Installs take a deposit (no more than one-third in Massachusetts) and the balance at completion. Card, check or bank transfer.
