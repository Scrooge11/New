The 12-point checklist we run at your pool

Florida pools fight sun, storms, and constant growth. Our checklist covers cleaning, chemistry, and equipment on every visit — because skipping any one of the three is how pools turn green.

Cleaning

  • Skim the surface and remove floating debris
  • Brush walls, steps, and waterline tile
  • Vacuum the floor as needed
  • Empty skimmer and pump baskets
  • Rinse the surrounding deck of debris we create

Chemistry & equipment

  • Test chlorine, pH, and alkalinity on site
  • Balance water with included chemicals
  • Check salt cell output on salt systems
  • Inspect pump, filter pressure, and timer settings
  • Check for visible leaks or early equipment wear

And before we leave

  • Latch every gate we opened — non-negotiable around kids and pets
  • Send your photo report so you see the result without walking outside

Good to know

Weekly service questions, answered

Do I need to be home during my weekly service?

No. Most of our customers are at work when we visit. As long as we have gate access, your technician completes the full checklist and sends a photo report when the pool is done.

What if it rains on my service day?

Light rain doesn't stop a visit — chemistry work matters most after storms. If severe weather forces a reschedule, we contact you first and make the visit up within the same week.

Are chemicals included in the weekly rate?

Yes. Standard sanitizing and balancing chemicals are included in your flat weekly rate, so there are no surprise line items on your invoice.

Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?

Service is month-to-month. We keep customers with white-glove work, not paperwork — you can pause or cancel with 30 days' notice.
