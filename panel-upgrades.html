Electrical panel upgrades in Westford and nearby towns

A 100-amp panel was fine in 1985. Add an EV charger, a heat pump and an induction range and it is not. We replace fuse boxes and undersized panels with a new 200-amp service, bring grounding and bonding up to code, and handle the utility, the permit and the inspection.

What is included

  • New 200-amp panel with copper bus and arc-fault and ground-fault breakers where code requires them
  • Meter socket, mast and service entrance cable replaced if undersized or damaged
  • Grounding electrode system and bonding (water, gas, rebar) brought to current code
  • Whole-house surge protection at the panel
  • Every circuit traced, labeled and photographed for your records
  • Utility disconnect and reconnect, permit and inspection
  • Drywall patched where the old panel came out

Signs it is time

  • Fuses instead of breakers, or a Federal Pacific, Zinsco or Sylvania panel
  • Breakers that trip when the dryer and the microwave run together
  • No empty slots for new circuits
  • Adding an EV charger, heat pump, hot tub or standby generator
  • Your insurer or a home inspector flagged the panel
  • Scorch marks, buzzing or warmth at the panel (call today, not next week)

How it works

  1. Call or send the form. Tell us what you have in mind and send a photo of the panel if you can.
  2. Site visit and written quote. We check the panel, the run and the finish work, then send a fixed quote.
  3. Work, permit, inspection. We pull the permit, do the work and meet the inspector.
  4. Walkthrough. We show you what was done, label everything and clean up.

FAQ

Electrical Panel Upgrades: questions we get

How long does a panel upgrade take?

Most are one day on site. Power is off for four to eight hours while we swap the panel, and the inspection is scheduled within a few days.

Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade in Massachusetts?

Yes. A service change requires an electrical permit and an inspection by the town wiring inspector. We pull the permit, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector.

Will a new panel lower my insurance?

Many insurers decline or surcharge homes with fuse boxes or known-defective panels. A new panel often removes the surcharge; ask your agent before and after.

Can you upgrade to 200 amps if the utility service is 100 amps?

Usually yes. We confirm the service drop with the utility and replace the meter socket and service cable as part of the job.
