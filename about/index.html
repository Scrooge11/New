Our story

Done-Right Beats Cheapest. Every Time.

Blue Water Pool was founded in 2026 on one observation: Palm Beach County has plenty of pool guys, and almost no pool service.

Why we exist

The gap between a clean pool and a cared-for pool

Ask homeowners here about their pool service and you hear the same stories: the tech who shows up whenever, the chlorine dumped without testing, the filter that hadn't been opened in a year, the invoice with mystery charges. The pool looks fine — until it doesn't, and then it's a green swamp and a $2,000 equipment repair.

Blue Water Pool Services was founded by Colin in 2026 to run pool care the way a great hotel runs housekeeping: on a checklist, on a schedule, with proof of work, by people who take visible pride in the result. We call it white-glove service because that's the standard — not because it costs white-glove money. It's one flat weekly rate, chemicals included.

We're deliberately a one-service company. We don't remodel pools, we don't sell equipment we don't believe in, and we don't take on more routes than we can run impeccably. We do weekly pool care in Palm Beach County, and we intend to be the best at it.

How we operate

Three commitments we make to every customer

The checklist is the contract

Twelve points, every visit, no judgment calls about what your pool "probably doesn't need this week." If we ever miss one, tell us and the visit is free.

Show up like clockwork

A fixed day, a consistent technician, and proactive communication when weather interferes. Reliability isn't a feature of the service — it is the service.

Prove the work

A photo report after every visit. You should never have to wonder whether your pool service actually happened — you'll see it, every week, from anywhere.

Our guarantee

If a visit isn't right, it's free

No fine print. If we ever leave your pool short of the standard on this website, tell us within 48 hours and that week's service is on us — and we come back to fix it.
