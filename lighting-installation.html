Lighting and ceiling fan installation in Westford and nearby towns

Good lighting is the cheapest renovation there is. We add recessed and under-cabinet lighting where there was none, hang fixtures on high ceilings, brace and wire ceiling fans properly, and set up dimmers that do not flicker.

What is included

  • Recessed LED lighting with slim wafer or can fixtures, laid out for even light
  • Under-cabinet, pendant and chandelier installation, including vaulted and two-story ceilings
  • Exterior, landscape, motion and security lighting
  • Ceiling fans on fan-rated boxes with proper bracing
  • Bathroom exhaust fans vented outside, with humidity sensors
  • Dimmers, timers and smart switches (Lutron, Leviton) matched to your bulbs
  • Wire fished with minimal openings, and drywall patched paint-ready

Popular projects

  • Kitchen: six to eight recessed lights plus under-cabinet strips on separate dimmers
  • Living room and basement: recessed lighting to replace one center fixture
  • Bedrooms: ceiling fans with light kits and wall controls
  • Exterior: front entry, garage floods and path lighting on a timer or photocell
  • Bathrooms: quiet exhaust fans that actually clear the mirror

FAQ

Lighting and Ceiling Fan Installation: questions we get

Can you add lights where there is no wiring?

Yes. We fish wire through finished ceilings and walls with small openings and patch them paint-ready.

What color temperature should I pick?

2700K to 3000K for living areas and bedrooms, 3000K to 3500K for kitchens and baths. We bring samples so you can see them in your room.

My dimmer flickers with LED bulbs. Why?

The dimmer and the bulbs are not compatible. A dimmer rated for LED loads, matched to the bulbs, fixes it.

Can you install a smart switch with no neutral wire?

There are models that work without a neutral, and we can often add one. We check the box before we recommend a switch.
