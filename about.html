About

A small company built on years in the trade.

Owner-operated on purpose. Fewer jobs at a time, more attention on each one.

JEMCO Contracting & Electrical Services is owner-operated. After years running crews and large projects, [Owner name] started JEMCO to get back to what he likes best: doing the work himself, for people he will see again around town.

That means a smaller crew, a direct line to the person responsible for your job, and the same face at the estimate, the install, and the walkthrough.

We do residential and light-commercial electrical, and we handle the remodel work around it so you are not coordinating three contractors for one project. Every job is quoted in writing, permitted where required, and inspected.
