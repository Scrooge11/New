Electrical repairs and troubleshooting in Westford and nearby towns

A breaker that keeps tripping is telling you something. We find the cause, fix it properly and explain what we found, with a clear first-hour rate and a written quote for anything bigger.

What is included

  • Tripping breakers, dead outlets and circuits, flickering or dimming lights
  • GFCI and AFCI breakers and receptacles that will not reset
  • Buzzing, warm or discolored panels and devices
  • Aluminum branch wiring repairs with approved connectors
  • Outlet, switch and dimmer replacement, including tamper-resistant and USB outlets
  • Hardwired, interconnected smoke and carbon monoxide detectors
  • Code corrections from home inspection reports, with a letter for the closing
  • Knob-and-tube assessment and replacement planning

Call today, not next week

  • Burning smell, scorch marks or a warm spot at the panel or an outlet
  • Sparks or a buzzing sound from a switch, outlet or the panel
  • A breaker that trips again right after you reset it
  • Lights that dim when the furnace, well pump or air conditioner starts
  • Two-prong outlets, no GFCI near water, or missing smoke and CO detectors

How it works

  1. Call or send the form. Tell us what you have in mind and send a photo of the panel if you can.
  2. Site visit and written quote. We check the panel, the run and the finish work, then send a fixed quote.
  3. Work, permit, inspection. We pull the permit, do the work and meet the inspector.
  4. Walkthrough. We show you what was done, label everything and clean up.

FAQ

Electrical Repairs and Troubleshooting: questions we get

Can you come the same day?

We keep room in the schedule for urgent calls and take emergencies first. For a burning smell or sparks, shut off the main if you can reach it safely and call.

Can you fix the items on my home inspection report?

Yes. We work from the report, fix what is real, explain what is not, and provide a letter for the buyer or seller.

My house has aluminum wiring. Do I need a rewire?

Usually not. Approved connector repairs at every device and junction are accepted by insurers and far cheaper than a rewire.

Do you charge a trip fee?

No separate trip fee inside our service area. The first-hour rate covers travel and diagnosis.
