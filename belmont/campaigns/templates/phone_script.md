# Phone follow-up (only after scrubbing against the National and Massachusetts Do Not Call lists)

Call by hand, never with an autodialer or pre-recorded message, between 8 am and 8 pm, and stop immediately if asked.

"Hi, is this {{first_names}}? My name is {{sender_name}}, I'm a local home buyer here in Belmont. I sent you a letter a few weeks ago about {{property_address}}. Is this an okay time for a quick minute?

(If yes) I'm not calling to pressure you. I'm just checking whether you or your family have given any thought to selling the house, now or somewhere down the road. ...

(If they're interested) Would it help if I came by, or spoke with a family member or your attorney, and put a no-obligation number in writing? What timeline would feel comfortable to you?

(If not) Completely understand. Thank you for your time, and I'll take you off my list."

Log the outcome in the dashboard (status and notes) right after the call.
