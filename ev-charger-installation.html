EV charger installation in Westford and nearby towns

A Level 2 charger adds 25 to 40 miles of range an hour instead of four. We run the dedicated circuit, install the outlet or hardwire the charger you chose, and make sure your panel can carry it before we quote.

What is included

  • Load calculation on your existing panel, the step most installers skip
  • Dedicated 40, 50 or 60-amp circuit on a breaker sized to the charger
  • NEMA 14-50 outlet or hardwired charger (Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint, Emporia, Wallbox, Grizzl-E and others)
  • Outdoor-rated installation for driveways, with in-use covers and proper conduit
  • Load-management chargers when the panel is close to full
  • Permit and inspection
  • Paperwork for utility rebates and the federal tax credit where they apply

Good to know

  • Massachusetts utilities have offered rebates on home charger wiring; we confirm what applies to your address before we quote
  • A full or 100-amp panel often still works with a load-managed charger; sometimes it needs an upgrade, and we tell you which before you commit
  • Two EVs? One 60-amp circuit with two power-sharing chargers is cheaper than two circuits
  • Hardwired is required for most 48-amp chargers; a 14-50 outlet lets you take a plug-in charger with you when you move

How it works

  1. Call or send the form. Tell us what you have in mind and send a photo of the panel if you can.
  2. Site visit and written quote. We check the panel, the run and the finish work, then send a fixed quote.
  3. Work, permit, inspection. We pull the permit, do the work and meet the inspector.
  4. Walkthrough. We show you what was done, label everything and clean up.

FAQ

EV Charger Installation: questions we get

Outlet or hardwired charger?

Hardwired is required for most 48-amp chargers and is better outdoors. A NEMA 14-50 outlet is the flexible option for a plug-in charger up to 40 amps. We size the circuit to the charger either way.

How long does the installation take?

Half a day for most garages. Driveways with a long underground run take a day.

Do I need a permit to install an EV charger in Massachusetts?

Yes. Any new circuit needs an electrical permit and an inspection. It is included in our price.

Can my 100-amp panel handle an EV charger?

Often yes, with a load calculation and the right breaker. If not, a load-managed charger or a panel upgrade solves it, and we price both options.
