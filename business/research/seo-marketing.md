## 2. What the SERP looks like

- **Local Services Ads (LSAs)** appeared in ~28% of 500 home-services searches and ~49% of city-modified searches ("hvac repair dallas"), and took position 1 every time they appeared (WebFX, 100 keywords × HVAC/plumbing/roofing/electrical/garage, July 2026) [13]. "Electrician Westford MA" is a city-modified query, so expect LSAs on top.
- **Map pack** appeared in 488/500 (98%) of those searches [13]. Google's stated inputs are relevance, distance, prominence; one vendor claims the map pack takes ~42% of clicks (unverified) [18].
- **Directories**: a non-Google index for "electrician Westford MA" surfaced HomeAdvisor town pages, Yellow Pages, BBB, BestProsInTown, Thumbtack and Experience.com, plus local firms Ryan Gath Electric (288 Littleton Rd), Valcourt Electric, Prime Electrical Services, Klostermann Electric, Lifestyles Electric and VMA Electric [14][15]. HomeAdvisor pros shown had only 3–5 reviews (4.9–5.0) [15] — the local review bar is low.
- **Review counts to beat**: Semrush's roundup puts top-3 local results at an average 561 reviews / 4.8 stars (undated, skewed by restaurants) [16]; Shapo cites ~47 reviews for top-ranking businesses vs ~39 for the average business and suggests 40–50 recent reviews as a practical target [17]; Hook Agency's rule: if the local top 3 have 40–60, ~75 wins [19]. YouGov found #1 listings had more reviews than #2+#3, all top-3 rated >4.1 [20]. Search Atlas (3,269 businesses, 2025) attributes 19.2% of ranking performance to review count, 26% within the top 10 (secondary report) [17].
- **Ranking factor weights** (Whitespark 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors, 47 experts, 187 factors, published Nov 2025): GBP signals ~32%, review signals ~20%, on-page ~15%, citations ~7% (secondhand summaries) [21][22][23]; primary category is the single most influential factor; review recency now beats raw volume; services listed on the GBP affect rankings [24]. Proximity to the searcher remains the factor you cannot buy — a Westford address will rank in Westford/Chelmsford/Littleton and fade toward Lowell/Concord.

## 3. Google Business Profile best practices

- **Categories**: primary "Electrician"; add only categories you actually perform (up to 9 secondary): Electrical installation service, Electric vehicle charging station contractor, Lighting contractor; for the remodel line, check the live selector for Remodeler / Kitchen remodeler / Bathroom remodeler / General contractor [25][26]. Don't broaden to "sound full-service" — it dilutes "electrician near me" relevance [25].
- **Services**: list each job as its own line (Google's own examples: installing outlets/switches, installing an EV charger, installing a fan, replacing/upgrading a panel) [27]; a dedicated "EV Charger Installation" line matches long-tail searches that "Electrical Services" won't [26].
- **Service-area setup**: hide the address if customers don't visit (home-based business); Google still requires a real verifiable address; up to ~20 service areas [36][37]. Evidence on whether hiding hurts rankings is mixed [38].
- **Photos**: Google's data — profiles that add photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks; 90% of people are likelier to visit a business with photos [28]. Real job photos (clean panel work, finished installs) beat stock; JPG/PNG, 10 KB–5 MB, ~720×720 [28]. Cadence used by agencies: several per month, every job if possible.
- **Posts**: weekly (posts expire after 7 days but the freshness signal persists); monthly is the floor [25][26].
- **Q&A**: seed and answer common questions yourself (permits, EV rebates, emergency hours) — I could not re-verify Google's current Q&A policy text before the search budget ran out; confirm in the help center.
- **Reviews**: ask same-day at job close (QR on invoice or text ~2 hours after completion) [26]; recency matters more than totals — 40 reviews with several from last month can outrank 150 stale ones [26]; reply to every review within ~1 day — 89% of consumers expect responses to both positive and negative reviews [29]; 60%+ of consumers will leave a review when followed up with a link (Podium via Contrary) [30]; 96% are open to writing reviews but only 29% did last year (BrightLocal 2025) [29]. Velocity target: match the local top-3 count within 6–12 months, then steady 2–4/month.
- **Policy**: Google treats incentivized reviews (discounts, gifts, payment) as fake engagement and removes them; "review gating" (selectively soliciting happy customers / suppressing negatives) is prohibited; a 2026 summary adds bans on review quotas, staff incentives tied to review volume, requesting specific content, and asking for reviews on-site — verify against the live policy [31][32][33]. The FTC Consumer Review Rule (16 CFR 465, effective Oct 21, 2024) bans fake/AI reviews and sentiment-conditioned incentives; penalties up to ~$51,744–$53,088 per violation; FTC sent warning letters to 10 companies Dec 22, 2025 [34][35]. Honest, unconditional review requests remain legal.

## 5. Review & reputation statistics

- 83% of consumers use Google to read local reviews (Yelp 44%, Facebook 40%, YouTube 34%); 74% use 2+ platforms, 34% use 3+ (BrightLocal 2025) [29].
- 71% won't consider a business rated under 3 stars (BrightLocal 2024) [79]; 78% won't consider under 4 stars (Semrush roundup) [16]. In 2025 consumers became "more lenient" on review counts and recency [29].
- Each extra Yelp star correlates with +5–9% revenue (HBR/Luca); businesses that respond to reviews see ~49% more spend (Womply, secondary) [80].
- Reviews influenced 88% of consumers discovering a local business (Podium 2021) [81].
- **Referral share** (trades): word of mouth 79%, online search 62%, repeat use 54% among homeowners choosing a roofer; 67% rate reviews extremely/very important; trust sources: referrals 88%, reviews 74% (Roofing Contractor 2025 survey) [82]. HVAC shoppers (n=422, 2025): 39% start on Google, 52% judge quality by reviews, 33% friend recommendation [83]. 85% of homeowners say a friend/family recommendation is what gets them to try a new home-service company (GKV) [84]; 52% of small businesses call referrals their top source [85]. Planning assumption: 40–60% referral/repeat in years 1–2, falling as search grows.

## 6. Website & technical SEO

- **Core Web Vitals** (75th percentile, mobile): LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms (replaced FID Mar 2024), CLS ≤0.1 [86]. Keep the site light: one hero image, compressed job photos, click-to-call sticky button.
- **Schema**: `Electrician` (a `LocalBusiness` subtype) with `areaServed`, `telephone`, `openingHours`, `hasOfferCatalog`/`Service` per service; structured data doesn't guarantee rankings [87]. `FAQPage` rich results were restricted to government/health sites in Aug 2023 and removed entirely May 7, 2026 — markup is harmless but earns no rich result; keep FAQs as on-page content [88][89].
- **Service-area pages vs doorway risk**: Google's spam policy defines doorway abuse as pages/domains targeting specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page, or "substantially similar pages that are closer to search results than a clearly defined, browseable hierarchy" [90]; John Mueller called 1,300 "keyword + city" pages a doorway strategy (2019, secondhand) [91]. Safe pattern: ~8–10 town pages, each with unique content (local permits/inspector, utility rebates, completed-job photos, town-specific FAQs, ≥300 unique words), not a city-swapped template [92].
- **Citations/NAP**: #1-ranked businesses averaged 86 citations vs 75 for #10; 18% of tradesmen are missing from the most popular citation sites (vs 5% overall) [93]. Core US set: Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Facebook, BBB, Yellow Pages, Manta, MapQuest, Foursquare, Angi, Houzz, ChamberofCommerce.com, Nextdoor [93][94][95]; one vendor found cleanup beyond the top ~30 directories gave no further lift [95]. Keep one exact NAP string everywhere (suite/phone formatting included).
- **Link/listing opportunities**: Generac Authorized Dealer (training, tech ID, service evaluation, parts kit ~$3K, reported ~$10K/yr purchase minimum — forum reports) [96][97]; ChargePoint Training & Certification (licensed electrician + online course; non-certified installs void warranty) [98][99]; Qmerit installer network (licenses, GL/WC/auto insurance, background checks; 1,500 contractors / 24,000 electricians) [100]; Tesla Certified Installer (application, Partner Dashboard onboarding; US specifics unverified) [101]; Greater Lowell Chamber dues $349/yr for 1–5 employees [102]; Nashoba Valley Chamber (Shirley, 17 towns; 15% discount for Westford Business Association members, 2018) [103]; Westford Business Association (re-formed, 2025 board) [104]; WestfordCAT business sponsorship [105]; youth sports/PTO sponsorships and Mass Save/utility contractor directories.

## 7. Content ideas with demonstrated demand

1. "200 amp panel upgrade cost in Massachusetts" — MA ranges $1,800–3,500 panel-only / $4,000–8,000 full service upgrade [46], $5,000–8,000 Boston-area [47]; "200 amp panel upgrade" ~14,500 searches/mo (vendor) [8].
2. "Signs you need a panel upgrade" (tripping breakers, hot panel, 60-amp/100-amp service, FPE panels) [56].
3. "EV charger installation cost + permits in MA" — Level 2 needs a wiring permit and licensed electrician; permit fees ~$75–300 [48][49]; national install cost $800–3,000 before hardware (EnergySage) [106]; 1.6M US home chargers, +38% YoY (DOE via vendor) [107]. Include rebate explainer: Mass Save Level 2 rebate $300 (up to $600 moderate-income; third-party listing, verify) [50], National Grid 2025 wiring rebate $700–1,400 (closed Mar 31, 2026; check for 2026 program) [51], Eversource program changed after Oct 17, 2025 DPU orders [108], EnergySage MA incentives guide [109].
4. "Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?" (load calculation, energy-management alternatives) [8].
5. "What size generator do I need?" (14–18 kW for 1,500–2,500 sq ft, vendor) [57]; "Generac vs Kohler"; "transfer switch vs interlock"; storm-season checklists (New England outages).
6. Lighting: recessed lighting cost per fixture; landscape lighting; smart switches.
7. Remodel bundles: "kitchen remodel electrical requirements (MA code)", "finishing a basement in Westford: permits and electrical".
8. Town pages (Section 6) doubling as content; before/after job posts on GBP.

## 8. Budget % of revenue and timelines

- Home-services contractors: 5–10% of revenue under $1M, 8–12% at $1–3M, 10–15% at $3M+ in competitive metros (vendor) [110]; 8–12% general, 12–15% competitive [111]; 12–20% for businesses <5 years old vs 7–12% older [112]; growth-mode trades 6–8% (Hook Agency) [113]; WebFX 10–20% [114]. Cross-industry: Gartner 2025 CMO 7.7%, Deloitte/Duke CMO 9.4%, SBA ~7–8% for <$5M (secondary) [115]. **Suggested year-1**: ~10–12% of target revenue, front-loaded (website, GBP, LSA verification, photos), e.g., $300K target → $30–36K.
- **Timelines**: 28% of SEOs see ranking movement in month 1; 65% say leads start in months 3–6 (BrightLocal survey via Seologist) [116]; map-pack movement 30–90 days [117]; new domains add 2–3 months; settled visibility 6–12 months [118]; LSA verification up to 90 days [43]. Plan: LSA + GBP + referrals carry months 0–6; organic/map pack contributes meaningfully from months 6–12.
