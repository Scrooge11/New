# Competitive Landscape: Electrical Contractors and Remodelers around Westford, MA

Prepared for the JS Contracting & Electrical Services business plan. Research date: 2026-10-09.

**Service-area assumption.** This analysis assumes JS will serve Westford, MA and roughly a 30-mile radius (Chelmsford, Lowell, Littleton, Acton, Groton, Tyngsborough, Dunstable, Carlisle, Boxborough, Ayer, Harvard, Concord, and nearby southern New Hampshire such as Nashua/Hudson). That radius is an assumption, not a validated market boundary.

**Method and limits.** All facts come from web-search result snippets (Google Business Profile counts as relayed by aggregators, Yelp, Angi/HomeAdvisor, Houzz, BBB, Nextdoor, Thumbtack, company sites). Direct page fetches were blocked by the proxy (DNS failures on every competitor site), so website-quality notes are inferred from page URLs and snippet text. The search budget ran out before 15 follow-up queries could run; see "Gaps" at the end. Review counts drift constantly and differ by platform; treat them as order-of-magnitude. Self-reported figures are labeled "claims."

## 2. "Electrical plus contracting under one roof": how rare is it?

Only two firms in the area combine licensed electrical with remodeling, and both market it weakly:

- **Gulliver Contracting / Gulliver Electrical Contractors Inc.** (Westford, 43 Lake Shore Dr S). Electrical contractor opened 1996; the contracting division sells kitchens, baths, basements, decks, roofing. BBB A+ accredited since 2019 under Electrician + General Contractor + Remodel. Digital footprint is thin: the electrical Yelp listing is marked **CLOSED**, the contracting Yelp page is unclaimed, Nextdoor shows one recommendation, Yellow Pages shows zero reviews. This is the closest analog to JS and it is effectively invisible online.
- **Gilbride Electric Inc.** (Chelmsford, 21 Progress Ave). Houzz lists it as design-build (construction, electrical, remodeling); its own site is electrical-only (panels, rewiring, generators, new builds); est. 1994; **zero Houzz reviews**.

Everyone else splits the trades. Multi-trade home-service firms (MillTown, Landry, Heritage, 128, Village, Denommee, Paul the Plumber) bundle plumbing/HVAC/electric but not carpentry or remodeling. Local remodelers do not advertise in-house electrical: JP Works Remodeling (Westford; kitchens/baths/basements; serves Westford, Chelmsford, Littleton), Ted's Construction & Sons (Chelmsford), STL Remodeling & General Contracting (Groton; Acton, Ayer, Pepperell), LCB Construction (Groton). Mr. Handyman was founded in Chelmsford but is a handyman franchise, not a licensed electrical shop. Local Electrical Services offers "design/build" for commercial electrical only.

**Takeaway:** the positioning is genuinely rare, and the two incumbents who hold it have no review base to defend it.

## 4. Gap analysis

**What the top competitors do well.** Volume firms win on review scale (hundreds to thousands), 24/7 answering, financing, multi-year warranties, Generac dealer status, and a landing page for every town. The best owner-operators (JP O'Brien, Integrity, Spaulding, Harland, John Small) win on near-perfect ratings and neighbor word-of-mouth.

**Where they are weak.**
- *Price trust:* the most-reviewed firms carry recurring complaints about high hourly rates, surprise fees, and upsell pressure (Kuhlman, Sylvester, Heritage, Landry, MillTown, Giroux). MillTown sits at 2.5 on Yelp despite 45 years in business.
- *No written pricing:* nobody publishes a menu; only MJP even states a dispatch-fee policy.
- *No remodel capability:* electricians do not do carpentry; the two that do (Gulliver, Gilbride) have no reviews and a closed/unclaimed Yelp presence.
- *Thin local footprints:* most Westford-based shops (Carter, Valcourt, Gervais, Local Electrical, A-Phase, BLM&C) have no Google/Yelp review base, dated static sites (Spaulding's .php pages, Carter's testimonial page), or rely solely on Nextdoor.
- *Credibility noise:* MJP's self-contradicting claims and lead-gen intercept sites muddy the search results, which favors a firm with verifiable license numbers and real reviews.
- *Franchise gap:* Mister Sparky stops at Billerica/Bedford; Mr. Electric has no MA franchise here; Nashua is served from Dover, 50 miles away.
- *Capacity:* several well-liked locals are one-person or second-job operations (Harland's owner is a full-time firefighter; John Small "focuses on smaller jobs").

**Five positioning opportunities for an owner-operated electrician + contractor.**
1. **"One license, one crew, one permit-holder" remodel electrical.** Sell kitchen/bath/basement remodels and EV-ready garage conversions where the electrical, framing, and finish are coordinated by one owner; co-market with remodelers that lack electrical (JP Works, Ted's, STL). Gulliver and Gilbride hold this ground but have not claimed it online.
2. **Published, written pricing.** Post a menu for the ten most common jobs (Level-2 EV charger, 200A panel, generator interlock/inlet, recessed-lighting packages, smoke/CO replacement, hot-tub circuit), a no-dispatch-fee-if-we-do-the-work policy, and written quotes within 24 hours. This directly answers the fee and upsell complaints aimed at the volume firms.
3. **Review velocity to crack the local pack.** Target 50 Google reviews in six months and 150 within 18 months (the Integrity/Spaulding tier), asking on every job; pair Google with Nextdoor (where Westford neighbors actually ask) and Angi, and claim/verify BBB, Yelp, Houzz, and the Generac locator so the license and address are consistent everywhere.
4. **Electrification bundle for an EV-heavy suburb.** Panel-capacity audits, EV charger + heat-pump circuits, Mass Save/MOR-EV rebate paperwork, and generator interlock/standby options as one package; pursue EVITP/Tesla/Qmerit certifications (Wired and Denommee are the only local holders) and Generac Authorized/Select status (Spaulding, Elliott, Harland hold it; Westford's Evan's does service only).
5. **Owner-on-every-job with a response promise and a simple plan.** Same-day callback, a 48-hour slot for small jobs, "no commissioned technicians, no quotas," and a low-cost annual safety-inspection membership (no local pure electrician offers one; only 128's base+ exists). Extend the same promise to the Nashua/Tyngsborough side, which no MA franchise covers.
