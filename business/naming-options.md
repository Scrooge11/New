### Trademark

No live federal registration for JEMCO turned up in the trademark aggregators. A Malaysian excavator-parts company's "JSB / Jemco Sdn Bhd" application was abandoned, JEMCORP Ltd. of Ontario has filings of its own, and "JEM" has been registered since 1978 for electric energy meters, which is a different mark on different goods. The USPTO database itself could not be reached from here, so the direct search is still required. Unlike "JS" or "Mass Electric", JEMCO is a coined word, so it can be protected once it is in use. The nearest prior users in this trade, JEMCO Electrical Contractors in New York since 1990 and JEMCORP in New Hampshire since 2006, have common-law rights in their own markets. That does not prevent use in Massachusetts, but it argues for filing the logo (the name with the lightning bolt) as a design mark rather than the bare word.

### Registration

Jemco Contracting, Inc. is an active Massachusetts corporation. The Secretary of the Commonwealth requires a new LLC name to be distinguishable from names already on file. "JEMCO Contracting & Electrical Services LLC" adds three words and in practice is likely to be accepted, but it is the examiner's call. Two ways to take the risk off the table: file the entity as **JEMCO Electric LLC** (or JEMCO Electrical Services LLC) and register "JEMCO Contracting & Electrical Services" as its d/b/a with the town clerk; or reserve the full name first ($30 for 60 days), which gets the state's answer before anything is printed.
