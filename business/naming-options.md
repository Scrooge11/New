## Recommendation

Rename the company **Senarian Contracting & Electrical Services** and build it on the domain **senarian.com**. The name is the only candidate that is personal, completely unclaimed and ownable across every domain that matters, and it matches the plan's core promise: the owner on every job. The runner-up is **495 Electric & Contracting** if a regional name is preferred, and **Galvani Electric & Contracting** if the Italian heritage angle is preferred.

The current name, JS Contracting & Electrical Services, is legally available and has a workable domain, but the initials are generic, an unrelated J.S. Electric already operates in Massachusetts, and nothing about "JS" tells a customer who they are hiring.

## What was checked, and what still needs a human

Each candidate was searched against business listings, review sites and company websites in Massachusetts and New Hampshire, and against companies nationally that could cause confusion or a trademark problem. Domain availability was checked through a domain-suggestion service on October 12, 2026; a name it offers is available, and a name it withholds is almost always registered.

Three checks must still be done from a browser before any name is final:

1. **Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth business entity search** (corp.sec.state.ma.us). A new LLC name must be distinguishable from entities already on file. Optional: a $30 name reservation holds a name for 60 days.
2. **A registrar confirmation** (Cloudflare, Namecheap or Google Domains) before announcing a domain. Availability changes daily.
3. **A USPTO trademark search** for the chosen word. Descriptive words such as "contracting" and "electrical services" cannot be protected; a distinctive word or a logo can.

## What makes a good name for this business

- Memorable on the side of a van and in a neighbor's recommendation.
- Says who or what the company is: the owner, the trade, or the place.
- Ownable: no competitor in the service area with a similar name, and the .com available.
- Distinguishable on the state's records and clear of national brands in the same line of work (EV charging in particular).
- Fits the brand system already built: navy and electric blue, the bolt, the plug, Saira and Barlow type.
- Works for both halves of the business, electrical and remodel contracting.

**Pros.** Unique in search: Jeff is the only result for his own name. Personal, which is the strongest trust signal in a trade where the owner is on every job. Suggested forms: legal name Senarian Contracting & Electrical Services LLC; wordmark SENARIAN with "Contracting & Electrical Services" beneath it, the same structure as the current JS logo. Surnames can be registered as trademarks once they have been in use.

**Cons.** Spelling. People who hear the name will try Senerian or Sinarian; the fix is "Jeff Senarian" printed under the logo on the van and the website, and the redirect domains below. A surname brand is slightly harder to sell with the business one day than a neutral name, which matters only if a sale is the long-term plan.
